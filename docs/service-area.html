Serving the CSRA

Central Savannah River Area

The CSRA's warm, humid climate keeps pest pressure high nearly year-round. Fire ants and carpenter ants thrive in the region's soil and warm seasons. Termites are a serious concern for area homes given the Southeast's climate and older housing stock. Roaches, spiders, and the occasional wildlife visitor round out what most CSRA homeowners deal with. That's exactly why our service list is built around what actually shows up on local properties — ants, roaches, spiders, termites, and wildlife — with preventative plans to keep it that way.
