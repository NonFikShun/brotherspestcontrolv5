Your local, family-owned pest control team in Augusta

Augusta is the heart of the CSRA. The city has a big mix of homes — from older houses on raised foundations and crawlspaces to newer neighborhoods on the edges of town — and the warm, humid climate means pests are active most of the year.

Older homes tend to face termites, moisture in the crawlspace, and rodents finding their way in through gaps around the foundation. Newer homes more often deal with ants, roaches, spiders and mosquitoes. Either way, we start with a real inspection so we treat what's actually there.

Every visit starts with an inspection. We will re-treat for free between scheduled visits.
