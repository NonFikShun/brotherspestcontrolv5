Crawlspace & Attic

Crawlspace & Attic Services

Protecting your home from the ground up — moisture, air quality, and the conditions that invite pests in.

🧱

Encapsulation

Partial or full crawlspace encapsulation with a minimum 6-mil barrier, sealing out moisture, pests, and outside air. Most jobs are done in one day — free estimates.

❄️

Insulation

Attic and crawlspace insulation to help your home hold its temperature and keep energy costs down.

💧

Dehumidification

Humidity control that protects your crawlspace from the moisture that invites mold and wood rot, including dehumidifier and sump pump installation when needed.

🚧

Vapor Barriers

Vapor barriers (6 mil minimum) installed to keep ground moisture out of your crawlspace.

🧹

Cleanouts

Clearing out debris, old insulation, and pest damage so your crawlspace or attic starts clean.
