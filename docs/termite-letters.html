Which termite report does your closing need?

Most home sales and refinances in the CSRA call for a wood-destroying insect inspection, often called a "termite letter." The report you need depends on which side of the river the home is on. We're licensed in both states and provide both.

Homes in Georgia

We provide the Georgia Official Wood Infestation Report for homes in Augusta, Evans, Martinez, Grovetown, Appling, Hephzibah, Thomson and the rest of our Georgia service area.

Homes in South Carolina

We provide the South Carolina CL-100 for homes in North Augusta, Aiken, Graniteville and surrounding South Carolina communities.

New construction: pretreatments & 99B forms

Building a new home? We handle termite pretreatments for new construction and provide the 99B forms that builders and lenders ask for.
