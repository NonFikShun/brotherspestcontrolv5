Which Report?

The Right Termite Report for Your Closing

Many home sales and refinances in the CSRA require a wood-destroying insect inspection, often called a "termite letter." Which report you need depends on which side of the river the home is on. We provide both.

Georgia

Official Wood Infestation Report

For homes in Augusta, Evans, Martinez, Grovetown, Appling, Hephzibah, Thomson and the rest of our Georgia service area.

GA License #100964
South Carolina

CL-100 Report

For homes in North Augusta, Aiken, Graniteville and surrounding South Carolina communities.

SC License #B0004121

Pretreatments & 99B Forms
