# Brothers Pest Control — Template v5 Creative Brief
### Codename: "The Authority Standard"

## 1. Creative Concept

**"The Authority Standard"** borrows the *structural confidence* of a large, established regional pest control competitor — comprehensive service detail, trust-signal density, transparent process, FAQ depth, service-area precision — and rebuilds it honestly at Brothers' real scale. This is not a heritage story (v1), not an emblem/certificate concept (v2), not a tech/defense-system framing (v3), and not a friendly cartoon-warm site (v4). It is the "we run this like a serious professional operation" site: clean corporate-green-and-charcoal palette, a real 4-service quick-link row, a real 6-service catalog, real 9-city coverage, real 3-testimonial proof, and real credentials (GA/SC licensing, military/first-responder discount, family-owned/faith-driven values) — presented with the polish and completeness of a much bigger company, without a single invented claim, badge, or partner.

The tension that makes this concept work: **big-company structure, small-company honesty.** Every section a large regional competitor would have (why-choose-us grid, process steps, service-area breakdown, FAQ, testimonial carousel, footer trust bar) exists here — just filled with Brothers' actual facts instead of placeholder corporate boilerplate, so the comprehensiveness reads as *earned professionalism*, not inflation.

## 2. Site Structure — Multi-Page (6 pages)

Splitting it into focused pages lets each section get real depth — mirroring how larger, established sites organize information — while keeping any single page short and honest (no page has to stretch thin content to fill a slot). This is the same "comprehensive site" instinct as v2's multi-page heritage concept, but built around trust-signal breadth rather than heritage storytelling.

## 3. Color Palette

Green-forward, corporate-professional, deliberately distinct from all four prior variants (v1's palette is a muted rustic Pine Green/Barn Red; this is a brighter, more saturated "institutional" green paired with true charcoal/black — a different hue, saturation, and role entirely).

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Brand Green | Authority Green | `#0B7A4B` |
| Deep Green (gradients, dark sections, header) | Deep Field Green | `#0A5236` |
| Near-Black (text, footer, high-contrast blocks) | Charcoal Ink | `#15191C` |
| White | Pure White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Trust-Badge Navy (pulled from the real logo ring) | Emblem Navy | `#1F3864` |
| Accent Gold (star ratings, small highlights only) | Review Gold | `#D9A441` |
| Neutral Background | Fog Gray | `#F4F6F5` |
| Neutral Border/Muted Text | Slate Gray | `#5B6670` |

Usage rules: Authority Green + Deep Field Green (as a subtle gradient) carry the hero, section headers, and primary CTA buttons — echoing the competitor's green-authority feel without matching its exact hex. Charcoal Ink and Pure White do the heavy lifting for body content, keeping the "corporate document" legibility the concept needs. Emblem Navy is reserved specifically for trust-badge/credential elements (Licensed & Insured callout, footer trust bar icons) so the one non-green accent color has a direct, honest tie back to the real logo rather than being arbitrary. Review Gold is used only for star icons next to the 3 real testimonials — never as a badge color, since there's no real award/rating program to signal.

## 4. Typography

- **Headings:** [Archivo](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Archivo) — bold, geometric, wide weight range; reads as confident/corporate without being cold. Distinct from Fraunces (v1), Libre Caslon Display (v2), Chakra Petch (v3), and Baloo 2 (v4).
- **Body:** [Source Sans 3](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Source+Sans+3) — clean, highly legible, professional workhorse sans built for dense/document-style content (service lists, FAQ, service-area grids). Distinct from Inter (v1), Work Sans (v2), IBM Plex Sans (v3), and Nunito Sans (v4).

Pairing rationale: Archivo's bold weights give headers and CTA buttons real authority-site punch; Source Sans 3 keeps the higher content density (6 services, 9 cities, 6 FAQs) easy to scan — the pairing is built for a comprehensive, information-dense site rather than a single hero moment.

## 6. Logo & Palette Fit

The real logo (`assets/logo-badge.jpg`) is a circular emblem: navy ring, black brush-script "Brothers" + "PEST CONTROL" wordmark, white interior. It's a personal, hand-lettered mark — not inherently "corporate green" — so rather than forcing a redesign or recoloring the logo, the badge stays exactly as-is and is treated as a contained white circular unit that sits cleanly on top of the green/charcoal header and footer (white-on-dark badge placement is a standard, low-risk way to keep a warm/personal logo legible inside a more corporate-feeling shell). The logo's navy ring is then deliberately pulled out and reused as **Emblem Navy (`#1F3864`)** — the one non-green/non-neutral accent in the palette, reserved specifically for the credential/trust-badge elements (Licensed & Insured callout, footer trust bar). This gives the site's "corporate trust signal" motif a literal, honest tie back to the real logo instead of introducing an arbitrary third color, while the green-and-charcoal system does the heavier lifting of signaling "professional, established-feeling operation" that this variant's concept calls for.
